# Facilitator Playbook — Teach the AIDT Protocol in 2 Sessions

## Session 1 (90 min)
- Intro to Agents, Handoffs, Handshakes, Decisions.
- Scaffold a project; create A1/A2 with `make new-agent`.
- Do a mock handshake and draft `A3.decision.yaml`.

## Homework
- Each trainee runs a full loop on a small feature and submits their HH folder.

## Session 2 (90 min)
- Review homework; discuss drift and decision quality.
- Add CI validation and improve SOPs.
- Q&A: applying to real projects.
